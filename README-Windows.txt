VESPERA FOR WINDOWS
===================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example Documents\Vespera).
2. Double-click Vespera.exe.
3. Vespera opens in your web browser at http://localhost:8080.

Keep the black "Vespera server" window open while you use Vespera.
Close that window to stop Vespera.

Your accounts, games and uploads are saved in the "data" folder next to
Vespera.exe. Back it up to keep them safe. To move your Vespera to another
computer, copy the whole folder including "data".

The first account you create is the admin.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app
isn't signed by a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
Windows Firewall may also ask about network access: allow it on private
networks if you want other devices on your Wi-Fi to reach Vespera.

vespera-img.exe shrinks uploaded pictures so pages load faster. Keep it next
to Vespera.exe (Vespera still works without it).
